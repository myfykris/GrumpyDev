# Domain-driven design survey contribution

## Applies when

Apply this contribution when the project models a complex business domain with
domain-driven design concepts.

## Inspect

- Read domain language, context maps, aggregate boundaries, invariants,
  commands, events, repositories, and integration contracts.

## Ask when unresolved

- Which business invariants and shared terms define the project's bounded
  contexts and aggregate boundaries?

- Where do translations, ownership, transactions, and eventual consistency
  cross contexts?

## Record

Map accepted Domain-driven design choices to `Accepted decisions`,
binding boundaries to `Requirements` or `Constraints`, and material gaps to
`Unknowns`.
