# Data privacy survey contribution

## Applies when

Apply this contribution when the project collects, derives, stores, shares, exports,
retains, or deletes personal or sensitive data.

## Inspect

- Read the data inventory, purposes, classifications, consent and notice, access
  paths, retention, deletion, exports, subprocessors, logs, and backups.

## Ask when unresolved

- Which personal or sensitive data categories are collected, for what purpose,
  and under which retention and residency rules?

- How do deletion, export, consent changes, vendor transfer, and incident
  scoping work across every copy?

- Which runtime or deployment differences materially change future reviews:
  Processing and storage regions, subprocessors, tenant boundaries, backups,
  logs, analytics, retention, deletion propagation, access ownership, and
  disaster recovery copies?

## Record

Map durable Data privacy requirements and constraints to the matching doctrine
sections, runtime facts to `Runtime and deployment`, and material gaps to
`Unknowns`.
