# shadcn/ui survey contribution

## Applies when

Apply this contribution when the project uses or materially depends on shadcn/ui.

## Inspect

- Read the components configuration, registry sources, installed component source, Radix or
  other primitives, and styling setup.

## Ask when unresolved

- Which shadcn/ui setup, framework, registries, primitives, styling system, and
  local component modifications apply?

- Who owns registry trust, source review, upgrades, accessibility, design
  tokens, and merge behavior?

## Record

Map durable shadcn/ui version and behavior requirements to `Requirements` or
`Constraints`, lifecycle and deployment facts to `Runtime and deployment`, and
material gaps to `Unknowns`.
