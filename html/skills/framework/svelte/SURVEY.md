# Svelte and SvelteKit survey contribution

## Applies when

Apply this contribution when the project uses or materially depends on Svelte and
SvelteKit.

## Inspect

- Read Svelte, SvelteKit, adapter, Vite, and package versions plus route and rendering
  configuration.

## Ask when unresolved

- Which Svelte, SvelteKit, reactivity mode, adapter, host, route rendering, and
  service-worker choices apply?

- Where do load data, actions, sessions, authorization, environment values,
  invalidation, and errors live?

## Record

Map durable Svelte and SvelteKit version and behavior requirements to
`Requirements` or `Constraints`, lifecycle and deployment facts to `Runtime and
deployment`, and material gaps to `Unknowns`.
